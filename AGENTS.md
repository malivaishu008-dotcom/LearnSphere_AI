# Repository working agreement

- Keep all user-facing language supportive and non-judgmental.
- Never claim predicted marks are certain or diagnostic.
- Never ask students for third-party account passwords. Provider credentials belong only in server-side secret management or approved OAuth flows.
- Keep personal uploads out of git. Use fixtures with no personal data for tests.
- Run `python -m unittest discover -s 08_testing` before hand-off.
- Do not remove historical research evidence without explicit team approval.
